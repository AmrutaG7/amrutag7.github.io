My Journey Into Data Analytics

I began my career with a strong foundation in business and management, earning a Post Graduate Diploma in Management Studies (MBA). Early in my journey, I recognized the value of data in shaping effective business decisions, which sparked my interest in analytics.

This inspired me to pursue a Master’s in Business Analytics at the University of North Texas, USA. Since then, I’ve worked across banking, healthcare, and consumer goods, focusing on omni-channel marketing through campaign analytics, audience segmentation, and improving data workflows for business insights.

Currently based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, I thrive on solving data problems that combine analytical rigor with real-world business impact.
Authorized to work in Canada without visa sponsorship.
